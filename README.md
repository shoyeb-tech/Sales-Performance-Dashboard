# Sales-Performance-Dashboard

>Project Title
.Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

>Purpose
.The purpose of this project is to analyze sales data to understand revenue performance, customer behavior, product trends, and  growth patterns over time.
 This dashboard helps stakeholders make data-driven business decisions by providing clear and interactive insights.

>Tech Stack
.Power Query – Data cleaning, transformation, and preparation
.Power BI – visualization and dashboard creation

>Data Source
.Dataset imported from Kaggle
.The data contains information related to sales, customers, product categories, locations, discounts, and order details.

>Business Problem
.The business needed a clear and interactive way to monitor sales performance, customer segments, and product trends    to support faster and better decision-making.

>Goal of the Dashboard
.To provide a single, interactive view of revenue, customers, orders, and trends across time, location, age group, and   product categories.

>Walkthrough of Key Visuals
.KPI Cards: Quick overview of revenue, orders, customers, AOV, and discounts
.Bar Charts: Revenue by age group
.Column Charts: Revenue by product category
.Line Chart: Revenue trend over multiple years
.Map Visual: Revenue distribution by location
.Slicers: Year, location, and age group for interactive analysis

>Business Impact & Insights
.25–45 age group is the highest revenue contributor
.Electronics is the top-performing product category
.Sales show a steady growth trend over time
.Enables data-driven decisions for marketing, inventory, and customer targeting
